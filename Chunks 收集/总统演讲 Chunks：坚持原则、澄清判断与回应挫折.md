## Chunks 与使用边界

| Chunk | 中文意思与语气 | 原文例证 |
|---|---|---|
| bring down the temperature | 缓和紧张局面；给争论降温；中性／公开讨论。这里的 temperature 是冲突强度，不是实际温度；说话时还需把前面的口语长句整理清楚。 | bring down the temperature.（[Joe Biden 2024-11-07 · P009](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202400984/html/DCPD-202400984.htm)） |
| lay [something] to rest | 消除疑虑、结束争论，使某个问题不再反复被提起；中性偏正式。不等于每一种 solve；常配 doubts、fears、rumors、questions。原句用 lay to rest the question，宾语较长所以后置。 | I also hope we can lay to rest the question about the integrity of the American electoral system.（[Joe Biden 2024-11-07 · P010](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202400984/html/DCPD-202400984.htm)） |
| make every [day/moment] count | 让每一天／每一刻都有价值；充分利用时间；日常与鼓励性表达。count 在此是有价值、产生作用，不是计数；不是 every 后可随意装任何名词的万能结构。 | Let's make every day count.（[Joe Biden 2024-11-07 · P020](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202400984/html/DCPD-202400984.htm)） |
| keep the faith | 保持信心，别因受挫而放弃相信；鼓励／略带演讲色彩。faith 在此可指信心，不必指宗教；日常更直白可说 don't lose hope。 | And above all, we need to keep the faith.（[Joe Biden 2024-11-07 · P025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202400984/html/DCPD-202400984.htm)） |
| win or lose | 不论输赢；输赢都一样适用；日常／口语与书面。可放句首或句尾补充条件；不是句式骨架 only when 的同义替换，而是把相反条件都纳入。 | And it can be trusted, win or lose.（[Joe Biden 2024-11-07 · P010](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202400984/html/DCPD-202400984.htm)） |
| live up to [an ideal/expectations] | 达到理想、承诺或期待的标准；中性。不是住在某地；常说 live up to expectations 或 live up to a promise。 | We've never fully lived up to that idea, but we've never walked away from it.（[Joe Biden 2023-09-28 · P012](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202300848/html/DCPD-202300848.htm)） |
| at one's own peril | 这么做会自担危险／后果；带警告；正式／郑重警告。比 at one's own risk 更带危险感，不宜用于每件小事。 | We lose these institutions of our government at our own peril.（[Joe Biden 2023-09-28 · P060](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202300848/html/DCPD-202300848.htm)） |
| I'd be surprised if [S V] | 如果……我会很意外；间接表示自己觉得那不太可能；日常与工作讨论。if 从句的肯定／否定决定预测方向；原句的 anything different 表示预计别人也听到相同担忧。 | I'd be surprised if you heard anything different than the concern about: Are we okay?（[Joe Biden 2023-09-28 · P059](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202300848/html/DCPD-202300848.htm)） |
| for all its faults | 尽管它有种种缺点，仍然……；评价／口语书面均可。for all 在此表达让步，不是为了全部；换对象时可用 for all his/her/their faults。原文 pass 后附官方 path 更正，照录。 | For all its faults though, American democracy remains the best pass [path; White House correction] forward to prosperity, possibilities, progress, fair play, equality.（[Joe Biden 2023-09-28 · P104](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202300848/html/DCPD-202300848.htm)） |
| all kidding aside | 玩笑归玩笑，认真地说……；口语／对话与公开讲话。用来从玩笑切回认真话题；没有玩笑铺垫时不必硬用。原句后续 more ... as ... 是口语中的不整齐比较结构，不作为模板；这里只收话题切换词块。 | But, all kidding aside, I've never been more optimistic about America's chances in domestic and foreign policy as I am today.（[Joe Biden 2023-09-28 · P109](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202300848/html/DCPD-202300848.htm)） |

## 跳过与边界

- `You can't love your country only when you win. / You can't love your neighbor only when you agree.`：收集箱已有同一 You can't ... only when ... 骨架；保留旧例句，邻居变体不重复计数。
- `What truly matters is not which party controls our Government, but whether our Government is controlled by the people.`：与本批 The question is not whether ... but whether ... 同属重新定焦动作；保留一条代表，此句留作变体。
- `Make America great again / America first`：依赖特定政治运动的口号；不适合作为日常表达的默认学习项。
- `not simply because ... but because ... / not only ... but ...`：常见对比与成对连接，已有一般性教材；本批优先选更有独立使用边界的表达。

最长保留项是“重新界定问题”的半固定骨架，完整原句仅作证据；不把整段政治论证当一个 chunk。演讲有正式、鼓动和郑重语气，日常使用时应按对象与场景调低力度。
