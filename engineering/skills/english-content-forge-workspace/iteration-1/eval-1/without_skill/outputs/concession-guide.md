## 1. 什么是让步结构

**让步**表达的是这样一种逻辑关系：

> 两个事实同时成立，但**其中一个本应阻止另一个发生，却没能阻止**。

"虽然下雨，我们还是去爬山了。"——下雨是"阻力"，爬山是"结果"，让步结构负责标记"这股阻力失败了"。

英语让步表达的核心分歧只有一条：

| 分歧 | 问题 | 阵营 |
|---|---|---|
| **词性** | 后面接什么？ | 介词派 |
| **强度** | 反差有多强？ | 连词派内部差异 |

掌握这两条，整个体系就通了。

## 2. 全景图：两大阵营

```
                       让步表达
                          │
          ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
      介词派（+名词/动名词）          连词派（+完整从句）
          │                               │
   ┌──────┴──────┐        ┌────────┬────┴─────┬─────────┐
 despite     in spite    although  though   even though   while
 ("of" 可加)  of           (标准)   (口语/特殊)  (最强)    (多义，需语境)
```

**一句话记忆：**

- 介词后面只能跟**名词性的东西**（名词、代词、动名词、the fact that 从句）。
- 连词后面必须跟**完整的句子**（有主语、有谓语动词）。

这是全部考点中最重要的一堵墙。

## 3. despite 与 in spite of：介词阵营

### 3.1 基本用法

despite 和 in spite of **意思完全相同，可互换**。despite 更正式、更简洁；in spite of 更口语、稍显冗长。

**后面可以接四种东西：**

**① 名词**
> Despite the rain, we went hiking.
> In spite of the heavy traffic, she arrived on time.

**② 代词**
> Despite this / that / it all, he kept smiling.

**③ 动名词**
> Despite being exhausted, she finished the marathon.
> In spite of having warned twice, he made the same mistake.

**④ the fact that + 从句（介词阵营接入句子的唯一官方通道）**
> Despite the fact that it was raining, we went hiking.

### 3.2 两个词的分工

| | despite | in spite of |
|---|---|---|
| 语域 | 偏书面、新闻、学术 | 口语与书面均可 |
| 长度 | 短，效率高 | 略啰嗦 |
| 正式度排序 | in spite of < despite < notwithstanding |

### 3.3 致命搭配错误

```
✗ despite of the rain          ← 不存在这个结构
✗ in despite of the rain       ← 同上
✓ despite the rain
✓ in spite of the rain
```

despite 本身就自带 of 的功能，再加 of 属于双重介词叠加。

### 3.4 despite 后面能放从句吗？

直接放：**不能**。

```
✗ Despite it was raining, we went out.
✓ Despite the rain, we went out.
✓ Despite the fact that it was raining, we went out.
```

绕行方案只有两个：① 名词化；② the fact that。

### 3.5 常见名词化技巧

从句 → 名词短语，是介词阵营的核心改写技能：

| 从句 | 名词化 |
|---|---|
| it was raining | the rain |
| he was ill | his illness |
| she failed the exam | her failure in the exam |
| prices increased | the increase in prices |
| they were tired | their tiredness / being tired |

## 4. although / even though / though：连词阵营

### 4.1 基本结构

三者都是**从属连词**，后接**完整从句**（主语 + 谓语）：

> Although it was raining, we went hiking.
> Even though she was exhausted, she finished the marathon.
> Though he studied hard, he didn't pass.

### 4.2 强度差异（核心考点）

反差感从弱到强：

```
although < though < even though
```

- **although**：中性陈述反差，最常用、最安全。
  > Although he's 70, he still runs every morning.（平静地陈述）

- **even though**：强调"出乎意料"，反差感最强。
  > Even though he's 70, he still runs every morning.（哇，真没想到！）

- **even though 不可拆开写**：
  ```
  ✗ Even although he was tired, ...
  ✓ Even though he was tired, ...
  ```

### 4.3 though 的三重身份

though 是三个词中**最灵活**的，有三种用法：

**① 普通让步连词（= although）**
> Though it was late, we kept working.

**② 句尾副词（= however），口语高频**
> The exam was hard. I passed it, though.
> Nice flat, though, isn't it?

注意：句尾 though 前面通常有逗号，且**不能用 although 替换**——although 绝不能放句尾。

**③ 倒装让步（高级，= as 的让步倒装）**
> Tired though he was, he kept working.
> Strange though it may seem, I actually enjoy meetings.

结构：**形容词/副词 + though + 主语 + 谓语**。although 没有这个用法。

### 4.4 连词阵营对照表

| | although | even though | though |
|---|---|---|---|
| 引导从句 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 强度 | 中性 | 最强 | 中性 |
| 句尾副词用法 | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| 倒装用法 | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| 语域 | 中性偏正式 | 中性 | 最口语 |

## 5. while：一个词的三副面孔

while 是本套词里**最危险**的一个，因为它有三个意思，只有语境能判定：

### 5.1 三种含义

**① 时间："当…的时候"（同时发生）**
> While I was cooking, the phone rang.

**② 对比："而，然而"（两事物并置反差）**
> Coffee prices rose in the north, while they fell in the south.

**③ 让步："虽然"（≈ although，多用于句首）**
> While I understand your point, I don't agree with it.

### 5.2 如何区分

| 线索 | 判断 |
|---|---|
| 从句是进行时/持续性动作 | 时间义 |
| 主从句主语不同 + 并列反差 | 对比义 |
| 句首 + "我知道…但是" 的让步语气 | 让步义 |

**对比义与让步义的模糊地带**：很多语法学家认为 while 的"让步"其实是"对比"的弱化版。实际写作中，只要不是纯时间义，理解成"对比/让步"都不会错太多。

### 5.3 使用注意

- while 表让步时**通常放句首**；放句中容易被读成对比义。
  > While the plan has merit, it is costly.（让步：虽然计划有价值，但成本高）
  > The plan has merit, while it is costly.（倾向对比：计划有价值，而成本又高）

- **while 后面不能只跟名词**：
  ```
  ✗ While the rain, we went out.        ← while 是连词
  ✓ While it was raining, we went out.
  ✓ Despite the rain, we went out.
  ```

## 6. 强度层级与选择策略

把五兄弟（含 though）放到一条强度轴上：

```
弱 ◀──────────────────────────────────────────▶ 强

while(让步) ≈ although ≈ though  <  in spite of  <  despite the   <  even though
（中性陈述）                      （客观阻力）    fact that     （出乎意料）
```

**选择决策树：**

1. 后面是**名词/动名词**？→ despite / in spite of
2. 后面是**完整从句**？
   - 想表达中性让步 → although（或句首 while）
   - 想强调反差、出乎意料 → even though
   - 口语、或想放句尾 → though
3. 想接从句但坚持用 despite？→ despite the fact that（书面可，口语略笨重）

## 7. 位置与标点规则

### 7.1 从属让步从句的位置

**句首（强调让步）：**
> Although it was raining, we went hiking.
> **逗号必须有**（从句在前，主句在后时）。

**句尾（弱化让步，补充说明）：**
> We went hiking although it was raining.
> **不加逗号**（书面规范）。

**句中（插入，成对逗号）：**
> The plan, though expensive, is worth trying.

### 7.2 despite / in spite of 短语的位置

规则相同：句首加逗号，句尾不加。

> Despite the rain, we went hiking.
> We went hiking despite the rain.

### 7.3 判断逗号的通用心法

> **先出现的让步成分 → 后面跟逗号；后出现的 → 前面不加。**
> 例外：插入式（主句中间）用成对逗号。

## 8. 高频错误清单

这是考试和写作中最常丢分的地方，逐条自查：

**E1. despite of 综合症**
```
✗ Despite of his illness, he came.
✗ In despite of his illness, he came.
✓ Despite his illness, he came.
```

**E2. despite 后直接接从句**
```
✗ Despite he was ill, he came.
✓ Despite his illness, he came.           （名词化）
✓ Despite being ill, he came.             （动名词）
✓ Despite the fact that he was ill, he came.（the fact that）
✓ Although he was ill, he came.           （换连词）
```

**E3. although 后接名词短语**
```
✗ Although the rain, we went out.
✓ Although it was raining, we went out.
✓ Despite the rain, we went out.
```

**E4. even although**
```
✗ Even although she tried, she failed.
✓ Even though she tried, she failed.
```

**E5. although 放句尾当 however**
```
✗ It was hard. I passed, although.
✓ It was hard. I passed, though.
✓ It was hard; however, I passed.
```

**E6. while 后接名词**
```
✗ While the noise, she studied.
✓ Despite the noise, she studied.
✓ While the neighbors were making noise, she studied.
```

**E7. in spite 与 of 分家**
```
✗ In spite the delay, we arrived.
✓ In spite of the delay, we arrived.
```

**E8. 让步从句用逗号连接两个独立句**
```
✗ Although it rained, but we went out.   ← although 和 but 只能留一个
✓ Although it rained, we went out.
✓ It rained, but we went out.
```

## 9. 进阶与替代表达

### 9.1 更高级的介词让步

**notwithstanding**（极正式，法律文体）
> Notwithstanding the objections, the board approved the merger.

**for all / with all**（口语化、带修辞色彩）
> For all his wealth, he is not happy.
> With all her experience, she still made the mistake.

**despite / in spite of oneself**（"不由自主地"）
> She smiled despite herself.

### 9.2 更高级的连词让步

**whereas**（正式对比，弱让步义）
> Whereas the north saw growth, the south saw decline.

**granted / granted that**（承认前提）
> Granted that the data is incomplete, the conclusion still holds.

**admittedly**（副词，承认让步点）
> Admittedly, the sample was small. The trend, however, is clear.

### 9.3 -ever 让步结构（同族扩展）

"不管/无论"式的让步，可视为 even though 的泛化版：

| 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| no matter + 疑问词 | No matter how hard he tried, he failed. |
| 疑问词 + -ever | However hard he tried, he failed. |
| whatever / whichever | Whatever you say, I won't change my mind. |
| whether ... or ... | Whether you like it or not, it's happening. |

注意：**however 让步用法中不可拆写**（However hard ✗ How ever hard），且此时 however ≠ "然而"。

### 9.4 as 的让步倒装

与 though 倒装同构：

> Tired as he was, he kept working.
> Try as she might, she couldn't open the door.
> Child as he is, he speaks three languages.（名词前**不加冠词**）

## 10. 让步 vs 条件：even though vs even if

最后一组易混概念：

| | even though（让步） | even if（条件） |
|---|---|---|
| 事实状态 | 从句是**已成立的事实** | 从句是**假设，可能不成立** |
| 语义 | "尽管事实如此" | "就算是假设的极端情况" |
| 例句 | Even though it rained, we went out.（确实下了雨） | Even if it rains, we'll go out.（还没下，可能下） |

**快速测试**：把从句换成过去的事实还是未来的假设，就能判断该用哪个。

同理对比：

> Though he is rich（事实：他确实富）…
> Even if he were rich（假设：假设他富）…

## 11. 速查表

| 表达 | 词性 | 后面接 | 强度 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| despite | 介词 | 名词/代词/动名词/the fact that | 客观中性 | 正式；**绝不能**加 of |
| in spite of | 复合介词 | 同 despite | 客观中性 | 与 despite 同义，更口语 |
| although | 从属连词 | 完整从句 | 中性 | 最安全的选择 |
| even though | 从属连词 | 完整从句 | 最强 | 不能写成 even although |
| though | 从属连词/副词 | 从句（作连词时） | 中性 | 可作句尾副词、可倒装 |
| while | 从属连词 | 完整从句 | 中性偏弱 | 三义：时间/对比/让步 |
| despite the fact that | 介词+复合结构 | 从句 | 中性偏强 | 书面绕行通道 |
| notwithstanding | 介词/连词 | 名词或从句 | 正式 | 法律文体 |

## 12. 练习题

### A. 填入正确的表达（despite / in spite of / although / even though / though / while）

1. ______ the high cost, the treatment was worth it.
2. ______ she had trained for months, she still lost the race.
3. The restaurant was full. We got a table, ______.
4. ______ I respect his opinion, I have to disagree.
5. ______ he was only twelve, he could solve calculus problems.
6. Sales rose in Europe ______ they fell in Asia.
7. ______ the fact that the evidence was weak, the court convicted him.
8. Nice house, ______, the garden is tiny.
9. ______ all his efforts, the project failed.
10. ______ it was midnight, the streets were crowded.

### B. 改错（每句有一处错误）

11. Despite of the warning, he climbed the tower.
12. Although the rain, the match continued.
13. Even although she apologized, he refused to forgive her.
14. We enjoyed the trip in despite the delays.
15. While the noise, the baby slept soundly.
16. Although he was tired, but he kept working.

### C. 同义改写（保持句意）

17. Although it was snowing heavily, the flight took off.
   → 用 despite 改写（两种方式）
18. Despite his lack of experience, he got the job.
   → 用 even though 改写
19. It was expensive. People bought it anyway.（用 though 句尾副词连接）
20. While I admit the plan is risky, I believe it can work.（句首让步，换 although）

## 13. 练习答案与解析

### A. 填空

1. **Despite / In spite of** — 后接名词短语 the high cost，须用介词阵营。
2. **Even though** — "训练数月仍输"反差强，强调出乎意料。
3. **though** — 句尾副词用法，although 不能放句尾。
4. **Although / While** — 句首完整从句，中性让步；while 在句首可表让步。
5. **Even though / Although** — 十二岁会微积分是强反差，even though 最佳。
6. **while** — 欧洲涨、亚洲跌，两事物并置，是对比义。
7. **Despite** — despite the fact that 是介词接从句的官方通道。
8. **though** — 口语插入语，"不过话说回来"。
9. **Despite / In spite of / For all** — 后接名词短语 his efforts；for all 是高级用法。
10. **Although / Even though** — 完整从句；"午夜街上还拥挤"反差较强，even though 也成立。

### B. 改错

11. Despite of → **Despite**。despite 后不能加 of。
12. Although the rain → **Although it was raining**（或 **Despite the rain**）。although 须接从句。
13. Even although → **Even though**。没有 even although 这个词。
14. in despite → **despite** / **in spite of**。in despite of 是过时古语，现代英语不用；正确为 in spite of。
15. While the noise → **Despite the noise** / **While the neighbors were making noise**。while 是连词，不能接名词。
16. 删去 **but**。although 与 but 不能同时出现（中式英语高频错误）。

### C. 同义改写（参考答案）

17.
   - **Despite the heavy snow, the flight took off.**（名词化）
   - **Despite the fact that it was snowing heavily, the flight took off.**（the fact that 通道）

18. **Even though he lacked experience, he got the job.**（从句化：his lack of experience → he lacked experience）

19. **It was expensive. People bought it, though.**（句尾 though = anyway/however）

20. **Although I admit the plan is risky, I believe it can work.**（while 让步义 ≈ although，句首互换安全）

## 结语：三句话带走全部

1. **名词跟介词，句子跟连词**：despite/in spite of 后是名词性成分，although/even though/while 后是完整从句。
2. **despite 不加 of，although 不配 even，让步从句配 but 是双标**。
3. **强度排序**：although ≈ though < even though；语体排序：though 最口语，notwithstanding 最正式。

掌握这堵墙（词性）和这根轴（强度），让步结构就不再是语法的坑，而是写作中制造反差、推进论证的利器。
