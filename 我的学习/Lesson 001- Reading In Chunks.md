# Lesson 001: Reading In Chunks

Date: 2026-09-14

Focus:

- Familiar words with less familiar meanings.
- Common chunks that should be read as one unit.
- Short sentences that are hard because the chunk is missing.

## Core Idea

The smallest unit of reading is often not the word. It is the chunk.

```text
take issue with
call into question
stop short of
leave much to be desired
be resigned to
```

A fluent reader sees these as one piece.

## Today's Chunks

### 1. bear out

Meaning:

```text
support or prove that something is true
```

Example:

```text
The evidence bears out his claim.
```

Translation:

```text
证据支持了他的说法。
```

### 2. strike a nerve

Meaning:

```text
touch a sensitive point and cause a strong reaction
```

Example:

```text
Her remarks struck a nerve.
```

Translation:

```text
她的话戳中了痛处，引发了强烈反应。
```

### 3. draw criticism

Meaning:

```text
attract criticism
```

Example:

```text
The new policy drew criticism from teachers.
```

Translation:

```text
新政策招致了老师们的批评。
```

### 4. take issue with

Meaning:

```text
disagree with something and object to it
```

Example:

```text
He took issue with my conclusion.
```

Translation:

```text
他对我的结论提出了异议。
```

### 5. gain traction

Meaning:

```text
start to get attention, support, or practical progress
```

Example:

```text
The proposal gained traction quickly.
```

Translation:

```text
这个提议很快获得了关注和支持。
```

## Mini Test

Translate these without looking back:

1. `The idea gained traction after the meeting.`
2. `His answer only compounded the confusion.`
3. `The data bears out her prediction.`
4. `I take issue with that assumption.`
5. `The joke fell flat.`

Then make one sentence with:

```text
take issue with
```
